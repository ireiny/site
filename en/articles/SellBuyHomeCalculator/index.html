2. What about tax on the sale?

Capital gains tax relief may be available

If you sell your primary permanent home and buy another to live in, you may qualify for full or partial capital gains tax relief, provided all legal requirements are met.

The amount reinvested matters. Even if you spend all the cash left after the sale, part of the gain may still be taxable.

Capital gains tax may apply

The tax depends on the buying and selling prices, documented expenses and your tax circumstances. Agency fees and certain documented improvements made within the previous 12 years may reduce the taxable gain.

3. Buying your next home

How will you use the new property?

The intended use changes the property transfer tax (IMT) and may affect capital-gains reinvestment relief.

If the new property will not be your main permanent home, the usual capital-gains reinvestment relief on the sale of your previous main home will generally not apply.

A 7.5% IMT rate may apply to non-tax-residents regardless of intended use. Legal exceptions exist, including where you become tax resident within the statutory period.
