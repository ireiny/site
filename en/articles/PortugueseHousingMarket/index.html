Portuguese Families & Housing Dynamics

Over recent years, domestic buyer dynamics in Portugal have adapted to changing interest rates and evolving preferences for energy-efficient living.

Local buyers increasingly prioritize high energy performance (A/B rated homes), private outdoor spaces, and suburban locations connected by train lines.
