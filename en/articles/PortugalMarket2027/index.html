Portugal Property Market in 2027: Buy Now or Wait?

Portugal’s property market is likely to remain expensive in 2027, although price growth may become more moderate. A countrywide collapse is not the baseline scenario, but overpriced homes in particular areas may require discounts. Here is what the data show and what could change the outlook.

The market today: three important facts

  • +16.5% year on year. According to Statistics Portugal (INE), the house price index rose by 16.5% in Q2 2026 compared with Q2 2025.
  • −6.4% transactions. Sales volumes fell by 6.4% in the same period. Fewer sales do not automatically mean lower prices.
  • €3,228/m². The median asking price on idealista in September 2026 was €3,228 per square metre, up 7.9% over a year. Asking prices are not the same as completed transaction prices.

Why prices do not have to fall

Housing supply in popular areas remains constrained. The IMF and OECD have identified structural obstacles including lengthy permitting, shortages of construction workers and slow delivery of new homes. This means weaker demand does not automatically produce a market crash. However, conditions differ sharply between locations.

One country, many different markets

The chart shows asking prices in September 2026. The gap between metropolitan Lisbon, the Algarve and more affordable regions is substantial. Within a single city, location, legal documentation, condition, energy performance and realistic pricing can all change the outlook for a home.

Three scenarios for 2027

Base case
+2–6%

Moderate nationwide price growth. Supply constraints provide support, but buyers become more selective about price and quality.

Downside
−5–0%

Stagnation or selective price corrections if financing becomes more costly, incomes weaken or demand slows markedly.

Upside
+7–10%

Faster growth if mortgages become more affordable while demand remains firm and available housing stays limited.

These ranges are my illustrative analytical scenarios, not an official INE or bank forecast and not a guarantee. Individual properties may perform very differently.

What looks more likely

  • The shortage of suitable housing in high-demand locations will not disappear within a year.
  • Prices and market conditions will continue to vary widely across neighbourhoods and properties.
  • Realistic pricing, property condition and complete documentation will matter greatly to a successful sale.

What remains uncertain

  • The path of interest rates and mortgage terms in 2027.
  • Economic growth, household purchasing power and external risks.
  • Regulatory changes and how quickly they are implemented. Always check current rules before a transaction.

Should you buy in 2027 or wait?

If you plan to live in a property for many years, the priority is not guessing the cheapest month. Focus on finding a suitable home at a fair price with affordable monthly payments. Compare properties, allow for taxes and repairs, obtain legal advice and assess financing early. If repayments would stretch your finances, do not rush.

What if you are selling?

Overpricing is less likely to be forgiven when buyers are selective. Research competing listings, check documents and prepare the property before going to market. A clear strategy can be more effective than simply posting an ambitious asking price.

My takeaway

I expect a more selective market in 2027 rather than another year of uniform rapid growth. Well-positioned, properly priced properties may continue to attract buyers, while overpriced homes may stay on the market longer. Decisions should be based on the specific property, not a single national percentage.
