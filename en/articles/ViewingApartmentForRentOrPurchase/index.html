Preparing for an Apartment Viewing: Renting or Buying

What to check before making a decision

You can tick off each point as you check it during your viewing.

I would start with the area, not the apartment itself. You can explore the neighbourhood before the viewing using Google Maps and Street View, then check the journey to work, school or other places you visit regularly. But photos cannot tell you how an area really feels. If possible, go for a walk there yourself. Google Maps.

If the location works for you, it is time to look at the building and the apartment.

What to check before making a decisionChecked: 0 of 20

1. Location and neighbourhood

2. Building and shared areas

If you have a choice, visit on a rainy day or in winter. Dampness and insulation problems can be easier to spot then.

3. Inside the apartment

4. Mobile signal and internet

A little tip

Ask as many questions as you need. With permission from the owner or occupants, take photos and short videos so you can compare different apartments later. If you have a chance to chat with the neighbours, do. They may know things about the building that are not obvious during a viewing.

This checklist helps you prepare for a viewing. It does not replace professional technical or legal checks before signing an agreement.
